## Mini self-test assignment

In `m0_practice.py`, independently train a model to learn **`y = 2x + 1`**.

Use these exact training examples:

```text
x: -2, -1, 0, 1, 2
y: -3, -1, 1, 3, 5
```

1. Create floating-point input and target tensors, each shaped **`(5, 1)`**.
2. Use **`nn.Linear(1, 1)`**; learn its weight and bias rather than assigning the answer directly.
3. Use **`nn.MSELoss()`** and **SGD with learning rate `0.05`**.
4. Train for **300 updates**, using all five examples each time. Include clearing gradients, prediction, loss calculation, backpropagation, and the optimizer update.
5. Print the loss before and after training, the final weight and bias, and predictions for new inputs **3 and 4** evaluated without gradient tracking.

Success checklist:

- Final training loss is **below `0.001`**.
- Learned weight is close to **2** and bias is close to **1**.
- Predictions for 3 and 4 are within **`0.1`** of **7 and 9**, respectively.
- Comments at the bottom explain why the inputs have shape `(5, 1)`, how `loss.backward()` and `optimizer.step()` work together, and why gradients must be cleared between updates.

When finished, share your output and say **“Review my M0 assignment.”** We will review your code and explanations to assess readiness for M1.
